# Decision Systems

Independent research and development in quantitative decision systems, statistical modeling, and decision engineering.

## Areas of Interest

- Decision theory and optimization
- Statistical and machine learning systems
- Rules-based and probabilistic decisioning
- Policy evaluation and causal inference
- Systems architecture and automation
- Decision-making under uncertainty

## Objective

To explore how statistical models, computational methods, and decision policies can be integrated into reliable, interpretable, and scalable systems.
